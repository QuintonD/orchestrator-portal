# Community code of conduct

We want participation in Orchestrator to be safe, constructive, and welcoming.

Contributors are expected to be respectful, assume good faith while evaluating evidence, give actionable feedback, credit others’ work, protect private user data, and make space for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate disclosure of private information, sustained disruption, and bad-faith security testing are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact method on their GitHub profile. The maintainer may edit or remove content, close participation, temporarily restrict access, or permanently ban a participant when necessary. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical and conflicts of interest will be disclosed or delegated.
